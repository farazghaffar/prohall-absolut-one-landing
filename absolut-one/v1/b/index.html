How to use

Your Absolut One routine

One spray, three ways to use it. Apply to damp or dry hair and style as desired.

  1. On damp hair

    Spray through towel-dried hair as a leave-in treatment.

  2. On dry hair

    Mist over dry hair as a finishing spray, or on the ends as a split-end conditioner.

  3. Style

    Style as desired: blow dry, flat iron or let it air dry.

Good to know: one 200 ml bottle provides about 2 months of treatment for medium to long hair. Always read and follow the directions on the product label.
